Living 3D creatures, not cute ones

All built in CSS 3D. They live through gesture rather than cuteness: each turns toward your pointer, leans in when you come close, looks around on its own when you stop moving, hops when pressed and spins when dragged. Faces stop at two eyes, and some, like the candle and the quill, have no face at all. The notch above each card shows it at real size.

Pick: ⑥ Candle, then ① Inchworm

The candle has no face at all and is still the most alive: the longer you focus, the taller and stiller the flame; on a break only an ember is left. It reads well even at 20px. The inchworm is the highlighter line come alive, segment by segment, but the research warns that a creature invites eyes, and eyes put it in a crowded genre of notch pets, so it should keep its single dot.
