Every mascot candidate, alive

Forty-three candidates in five sets, all on one page. The creatures breathe, look around on their own when you stop moving, lean in when you come close, hop when pressed and spin when dragged, even the small ones sitting in the notch. One state bar below changes all of them at once.

  • Armillary ringsThe research’s first pick: cheap to build in SwiftUI and CSS 3D, readable at 20px, and no face to resemble anyone’s character.Abstract 3D, no. 2
  • CandleNo face, yet the most alive. The flame grows tall and calm as you focus and shrinks to an ember on a break.Living 3D, no. 6
  • InchwormThe highlighter line come alive. Keep its single dot: two eyes would put it among the crowd of notch pets.Living 3D, no. 1
  • Highlighter ribbonOur two colours twisting in 3D. Better as the rings’ colour or trail than on its own.Abstract 3D, no. 3
