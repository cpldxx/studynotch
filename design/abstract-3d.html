Abstract and alive: eight 3D creatures

No objects and no faces: these live through shape, light and motion alone, borrowing the language of Apple’s glowing orb, activity rings, wallpaper ribbons and layered plates. Each one breathes, turns toward your pointer, leans in when you come close, looks around on its own when you stop moving, hops when pressed and spins when dragged. The state buttons change colour and behaviour together.

Pick: ② Armillary rings, coloured by ③ the highlighter ribbon

The research points to the rings: flat circles are the cheapest shape to build in both SwiftUI and CSS 3D, they still read as one or two rings and a core of light at 20px, and with no eyes there is no face to resemble anyone else’s character. They recall both Apple’s activity rings and an old armillary sphere, so the Apple feel and the academic feel share one shape, and the outer ring doubles as the progress indicator. The ribbon is better as the rings’ colour or trail than on its own.
