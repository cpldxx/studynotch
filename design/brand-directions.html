Eleven static marks, for reference

These are logos rather than creatures: marks that can show a state without a face, grouped as Apple-tidy shapes, school and library emblems, and single letters. The living versions of their best ideas are on the other sheets (the line became the inchworm, the owl the statue, the lamp the candle, the nib the quill). Colours are only our ink, paper and highlighter; the black notch above each card shows the mark at real size (18px).

As marks: ① Living line in the notch, ③ One page as the app icon

  • The notch ear is 18px, so every drawing shrinks; a single line is the clearest thing at that size and, like Apple’s Dynamic Island, shows state through motion.
  • The highlighter line already is what the product does; it needs no explaining.
  • The academic feel can come from the type (serif headings) and small touches like ④ the seal.
Drafts. Before any of these becomes a real logo, check for trademarks with the same name or a similar shape.
