Alive, not cute: eight flat creatures

Feeling alive comes from motion more than from a face. These breathe, blink, watch your pointer, glance around on their own when you stop moving, and change their gestures with each state. Cheeks, big smiles and round bodies are left out. The black notch above each card shows it at real size (18px).

Pick: ② Inchworm line

The highlighter line itself is the creature. What the product does (highlighting) and being alive are one body, so it needs no explanation, and with a single dot for an eye it stays tidy. It reads clearly as one line in an 18px notch and does not overlap other notch apps’ characters. ① Notch eyes is the most Apple-like, but eyes in the notch are already used by several other notch apps.
